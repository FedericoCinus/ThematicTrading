# Thematic ETFs — Overview Table

Reference table of thematic ETFs grouped by theme. For each fund:

- **Birth (Inception)** — the ETF's launch date (`YYYY-MM-DD`, or `YYYY-MM` where the day is uncertain).
- **Peak (Price ATH)** — approximate date of the fund's all-time-high *price*. Many speculative-growth funds peaked in the **Feb 2021** / **late-2021** bubble; the 2024–2026 AI / defense / power rally pushed several others to *new* all-time highs (≈2026).
- **Top Constituent Tickers** — most recent published top holdings (data ≈ Apr–Jun 2026). For active/equal-weight funds these rotate frequently. Foreign listings are shown with their local/primary ticker (e.g. `0700` = Tencent HK, `7974` = Nintendo Tokyo, `300750` = CATL Shenzhen, `688256` = Cambricon Shanghai).

> `~` / `(approx)` marks values that could not be pinned to an exact day. Compiled from web research; verify against issuer fact sheets before any production use.

### Reading the "Peak" column

Three broad cohorts emerge:

- **2021 bubble peakers (never recovered):** ARKK, ARKW, ARKF, WCLD, KWEB, CQQQ, MCHI, ARKG, GNOM, KARS, LIT, CNBS, MSOS, YOLO, FINX, AWAY. These topped in **Feb–Nov 2021** and remain well below those highs.
- **New all-time highs in the 2024–2026 AI / defense / power / uranium rally:** ARTY, AIQ, QTUM, ARKQ, BOTZ, ROBO, SKYY, ITA, PPA, XAR, SHLD, UFO, IFRA, PAVE, NLR, URA, URNM, PHO, FIW, HACK, CIBR, BLOK, LEGR, PEJ.
- **Idiosyncratic peaks:** ICLN/TAN (2008 nominal high), JETS (Jan 2018, pre-COVID), homebuilders ITB/XHB/NAIL (Nov 2024), gaming HERO/ESPO (late 2025).

> Tickers `ARTY` and `IRBO` are the **same iShares fund** (renamed from "Robotics & AI" to "Future AI & Tech" on 2024-08-12); the Feb-2021 peak belongs to the old robotics mandate. `ARKF` was renamed "ARK Blockchain & Fintech Innovation" and overlaps the Fintech and Blockchain themes.
